# Fast-Charging EV Battery Thermal Management System (BTMS)

An ongoing research project at **BITS Pilani, Rajasthan**, under the guidance of **Dr. Srinibas Tripathy**, focused on developing efficient Battery Thermal Management Systems (BTMS) for **Extreme Fast Charging (XFC)** of lithium-ion batteries.

### Current Work
- Reviewing and benchmarking advanced BTMS approaches using CFD-based research.
- Studying **liquid cooling, PCM, fin-enhanced PCM, NePCM, heat pipes, and thermoelectric cooling**.
- Evaluating thermal performance, temperature uniformity, cooling duration, system complexity, and scalability.
- Identifying promising configurations for experimental validation.

### Key Findings So Far
- Active liquid cooling can maintain module temperatures below **28°C at 5C** with very low temperature variation.
- A **4-annular-fin + PCM** configuration achieved a **55.3% peak-temperature reduction** at 3C.
- **4 mm NePCM with 1–2 wt% Al₂O₃** showed promising passive cooling performance.
- Multi-hybrid PCM + HP + TEC + liquid cooling demonstrated the highest thermal performance among the studied systems.

### Next Steps
- Develop and experimentally test optimized single-cell and 2×2 module prototypes.
- Validate CFD results under **2C, 3C and 5C** charging conditions.
- Extend analysis to full battery packs.
- Explore **NSGA-II-based multi-objective optimization** of thermal performance, weight, cost and cooling power.
